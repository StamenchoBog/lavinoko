## What the list already shows

- **The largest avalanche disaster in the country's records** (1956, 52 dead) came off Korab onto infrastructure and settlements, not onto skiers.
- **Avalanches reach roads** in the Radika valley (2019), which makes road authorities and civil protection natural partners.
- **Freeride activity on Šar Planina is producing incidents now** (two in winter 2025/26), and responsibility for off-piste terrain falls between the resort, private operators and the national park.
- **Warnings exist, but ad hoc.** The national park already posts avalanche warnings on Facebook after events, using the European scale. There is no regular bulletin, no defined regions, and nothing outside the park. The project should support and formalise this, not compete with it.
